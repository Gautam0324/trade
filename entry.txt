# ==============================================================================
# PART 1: CORE / CLASSICAL SETUPS (trader-entry & trader-exit)
# ==============================================================================

Trade Direction:
  - Long Side ONLY (v2 strictly plans longs; short selling is not supported).

1. Opening Range Breakout (ORB) [Default Setup]:
   - Opening Range Duration: First 15 minutes of session (9:30 AM to 9:45 AM ET).
   - Range Grid: Three 5-min bars (9:30, 9:35, 9:40) or fifteen 1-min bars.
   - Benchmark Level: Range High (highest high of the 15-minute window).
   - Trigger Condition: The first COMPLETED bar after 9:45 AM that CLOSES strictly above Range High.
     * Intraday wicks or high pokes above Range High do NOT count.
     * Candle close equal to Range High does NOT trigger.
   - Stop Loss Options:
     * `or-mid` (Default): (Range High + Range Low) / 2, rounded down to nearest cent.
     * `or-low`: Range Low, rounded down.
   - Target Options:
     * `2R` (Default): Entry + (2 * (Entry - Stop)).
     * `1R` / `1.5R`: Entry + (R * (Entry - Stop)).

2. Fixed Price Level Breakout:
   - Level Source: Scanner output (e.g., 20-day high, key pivot resistance).
   - Trigger Condition: First COMPLETED bar closing strictly above `--level <PRICE>`.
   - Stop Loss: Must be explicitly provided (`price:X`, `pct:X`, or `atr:K`).

3. Volume Confirmation Filter (Optional):
   - Flag: `--min-volume-x X` (e.g. 1.5).
   - Trigger bar volume must be >= X times the average volume of opening range bars.

4. Session Timing Constraints:
   - No entries before: 09:45 AM ET.
   - No entries after: 15:30 PM ET (Early close days: 12:30 PM ET).
   - Mandatory EOD Square-Off (`flat_by`): 15:55 PM ET (Early close days: 12:55 PM ET).


# ==============================================================================
# PART 2: INDICATOR-BASED ENTRY STRATEGIES (trader/strategy_engine.py)
# ==============================================================================

1. Strategy A: EMA 5 / 9 / 33 Channel Breakout
   - Indicators Used:
     * EMA 5 (Short-term momentum on Close)
     * EMA 9 (Fast trend baseline on Close)
     * EMA 33 Channel High (EMA 33 calculated on candle Highs)
     * EMA 33 Channel Low (EMA 33 calculated on candle Lows)
   - Entry Condition (Long):
     1. Current Candle Close > EMA 33 High (Bullish breakout above channel).
     2. Bullish EMA Crossover / Alignment: EMA 5 > EMA 9.
     3. Completed candle confirmation (no intra-candle entries).
   - Stop Loss Logic:
     * Structural stop at EMA 33 Low OR Dynamic ATR stop: Entry - (2.0 * ATR 14).
     * Stop loss = max(EMA 33 Low, Entry - 2.0 * ATR 14).
   - Target Logic:
     * Target = Entry + 1.5 * Risk (Minimum 1.5R target).

2. Strategy B: Intraday VWAP Pullback Entry
   - Indicators Used:
     * Session-anchored VWAP (Volume Weighted Average Price)
     * EMA 9 and EMA 33 (Trend direction)
     * ATR 14 (Volatility cushion)
   - Entry Condition (Long):
     1. Primary Trend Filter: EMA 9 > EMA 33 OR Price > VWAP.
     2. Healthy Pullback Proximity:
        * Recent candle low dips close to VWAP (distance between -1.0% to +0.8% of VWAP).
     3. Bullish Rejection Confirmation:
        * Completed candle closes bullish: Close >= Open AND Close >= VWAP.
     4. Extension Filter (Anti-FOMO):
        * Price must NOT be overextended (Distance from VWAP <= 2.0%).
   - Stop Loss Logic:
     * Stop Loss = min(Candle Low, VWAP) - (0.5 * ATR 14), rounded down.
     * Alternative: 0.4% below VWAP (VWAP * 0.996).
   - Target Logic:
     * Target = Entry + (1.5 * Risk).

3. Strategy C: Trend + Momentum Confirmation
   - Indicators Used:
     * EMA 9 & EMA 33 (Trend alignment)
     * RSI 14 (Wilder's smoothed Relative Strength Index)
     * ADX 14, +DI, -DI (Directional movement & Trend strength)
     * ATR 14 (Volatility buffer)
   - Entry Condition (Long):
     1. Trend Filter: EMA 9 > EMA 33.
     2. Momentum Zone: RSI 14 between 48.0 and 78.0 (Active momentum, not overbought).
     3. Trend Strength Filter:
        * ADX 14 >= 16.0 to 22.0 (Confirms a genuine trend, avoiding choppy market).
        * Directional Bias: +DI > -DI (Positive directional force).
     4. Candle Confirmation: Confirmed on completed bar.
   - Stop Loss Logic:
     * Dynamic Volatility Stop: Entry - (1.5 * ATR 14).
   - Target Logic:
     * Target = Entry + (1.5 * Risk) or (2.0 * Risk).

4. Market Regime & No-Trade Filters:
   - "TRENDING_BULL": EMA 9 > EMA 33, ADX > 22, RSI > 52  --> Valid for Long Entries.
   - "CHOPPY_SIDEWAYS": ADX < 20  --> NO TRADE (Prevents false whipsaw breakouts).
   - "HIGH_VOLATILITY": ATR > 2.5x 20-period average ATR --> Halt or reduce position size.


# ==============================================================================
# PART 3: ADVANCED DYNAMIC EXIT LOGIC (trader/exit_engine.py)
# ==============================================================================

1. Hard Initial Stop Loss:
   - Position closes immediately if Current Price <= Initial Stop Loss.
   - Stop is rounded down to the cent away from entry.

2. Multi-Stage Profit Booking & Breakeven Mechanism:
   - Target 1 (+1.0R):
     * When price reaches Entry + 1.0R: Book 50% partial profit (close 50% quantity).
     * Move Stop Loss of remaining 50% shares to Breakeven (Entry Price + transaction cost buffer).
     * Guarantees trade cannot turn into a loss once +1R is touched.
   - Target 2 (+1.5R to +2.0R):
     * Exit remaining shares at fixed 1.5R / 2.0R or allow trailing.

3. ATR Dynamic Trailing Stop (Ratchet Mechanism):
   - Activates when position is in profit (Current Price > Entry Price).
   - Formula: Candidate Stop = Peak Price - (1.5 * ATR 14).
   - Ratchet Rule: New Stop = max(Current Stop, Candidate Stop).
     * Stop only moves UPWARDS; it is NEVER loosened or moved down.

4. Peak Reversal Profit-Lock Exit:
   - If position is in profit and price falls by >= 0.2% from highest reached peak (Peak Price):
     * Immediate full exit to protect and lock accumulated intraday profits.

5. EMA Momentum Breakdown Exit:
   - If position is in profit:
     * If Current Price drops below EMA 5: Exit position.
     * If Fast EMA 5 crosses below EMA 9: Exit position (momentum reversal signal).

6. Intraday Session Square-off Cutoff:
   - NSE/Indian Market: 15:20 IST auto-squareoff.
   - US Market: 15:55 ET auto-squareoff.
   - All open positions are liquidated automatically, regardless of profit or loss.
